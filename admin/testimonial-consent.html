Consent to publish your comments

For patient feedback shared publicly on bagouri.uk

Thank you for offering to share your experience. Before anything you have written can appear on the website, we need your written permission. Please read this page carefully.

What would be published

  • Your comments, in your own words. We may shorten them, but we will not change their meaning.
  • Your first name only, or your initials — whichever you choose below. Never your full name.
  • The type of operation you had, and the month and year.

Nothing else. Not your date of birth, your NHS number, your address, your photograph, or any detail of your medical history beyond the operation itself.

Things you should know before you agree

  • Saying no changes nothing. Your care, your treatment, and your relationship with the team are completely unaffected by this decision, now or in the future.
  • You can change your mind at any time, without giving a reason. Contact us and it will be removed from the website.
  • A website is public. Once published, your comments can be read by anyone in the world, and may be copied or saved by search engines. We can remove them from our site, but we cannot guarantee removal of copies made elsewhere.
  • You will see it first. We will show you the exact wording before it goes online.
  • Please describe your own experience only, not what results other patients should expect. Every patient and every operation is different.
