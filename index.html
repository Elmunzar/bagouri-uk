If you are unwell. These pages are information, not urgent advice. If you are unwell, in severe pain, or worried about your wound, contact your GP, your hospital team, or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.

The information on this site supports the discussion you have had with your surgeon and physiotherapy team. It does not replace the advice given to you in clinic.
