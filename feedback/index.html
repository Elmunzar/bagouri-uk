If this is about your care, please use the right route — you will get a much faster answer.

  • A medical question, or something has changed: contact the team on 01977 747391, or your GP.
  • Urgent, or you feel unwell: NHS 111. In an emergency, 999.
  • A concern or complaint about your care: contact the hospital's PALS team — they are independent of the clinical team and there to help.
  • Public feedback about NHS care: the NHS website, or Care Opinion.
